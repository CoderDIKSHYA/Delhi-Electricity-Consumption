Electricity Call Center

To address power supply-related complaints proactively, several power distribution companies and state electricity boards have set up Electricity Call Centers. These operate 24/7 via telephone, fax, and email. Dial **1912** for power-related issues.

Complaints are registered, tracked, and forwarded to field personnel. Escalation mechanisms ensure resolution if not addressed in time. Call centers use advanced contact and process management systems, either in-house or outsourced.

States like Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka have implemented them, while Delhi and others are in progress — all aligning with regulatory service standards.
